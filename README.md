# Tom and Jerry

## About the Series

**Tom and Jerry** is a classic American animated franchise created in 1940 by *William Hanna* and *Joseph Barbera*. It centers on the continuous rivalry between a house cat named **Tom** and a clever mouse named **Jerry**.

> "An eternal game of cat and mouse where comedy and music take center stage."

---

### Main Characters

1. **Tom Cat** - A blue/grey domestic cat who is determined to catch Jerry.
2. **Jerry Mouse** - A small brown mouse who always manages to outsmart Tom.
3. **Spike** - An aggressive bulldog who often defends Jerry.

---

### Key Features

- **Iconic Slapstick Comedy** - Timeless visual humor that requires almost no dialogue.
- **Orchestrated Musical Score** - Combining jazz, classical music, and custom sound effects.
- **Award-Winning Legacy** - Won 7 Academy Awards for Best Animated Short Film.
